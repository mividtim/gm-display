Alignment grid
Fixed squares in projector pixels, for squaring the projector to the table. It does not move with the map — for a grid locked to the map use Map Grid.
Map Grid
The grid locked to the map — what tokens snap to, drawn on every screen. Calibrate it against a grid already printed on the map. Saved per map.
24
Grid is drawn on every screen — players see it even inside fog, so they can place and move tokens in the dark.
Calibration
With this panel open, arrow keys nudge the origin by 1px (Shift ×10, Alt ×0.1).
Cell Notes
Notes attached to grid cells, saved as markdown in your vault — one file per map. Edit them here or in Obsidian; both sides stay in step. Yours are never served to a player. What the party writes on their own copy appears underneath, and as a blue pip beside your gold one.
Legend
What the symbols on this map mean. Written as markdown in your vault beside the notes, so you edit it in Obsidian and it updates here as you save. Show it on the projector, and players can open it on their own screens.
Tokens
Tokens appear on the projector and on players' web view. Players claim one and propose moves; you approve. Positions, visibility, and the NPC list are saved per map; the roster is shared campaign-wide.
